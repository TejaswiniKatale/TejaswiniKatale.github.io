Love for Hobby

The purest love firstly felt ever, Scars fading slower, but the heart healing a bit faster felt as if the happiness flowing in his veins forever, Praying the earlier pain would be encountered never, Because the bleeding it caused was just so severe Dollar asking him a question, Will this feed your stomach forever? Confidently he answered, it will definitely satisfy my soul however! Smirking thinking as if the reply was just so clever!! But then this conventional society, started demanding for a different answer!! Trapped him in such an embarrassing situation, What he did to receive so much humiliation? Asking god was that just his infatuation? Adding but it was just his authentic creation! God smiling above answered, Dear, Surely It was not infatuation but it was neither love too
~Tejaswini

Puzzle

Millions of jigsaw boxes with hundreds of pieces Jumbling together trying to fit in, Standard RGB smirking they will definitely get in Shades of Red clustered to form a Sunset Shades of Green congregated to form a Forest Shades of Blue came together to form a Beach Abondoned black trying to convince,I can be; A black bird in the Sunset A black rock in the Forest A black ship on the Beach Trying to fade out by adding whites, All failed attempts to be that concise Having stamped by Still figuring out where to fit in, Returning to the box ready to quit in Bewilderd Shades of Grey waiting, aware that they just need one piece Asking Black would you like to try in, Joyous Black jumps in, Forming that Perfect Moon, Yet completing the puzzle of an Afternoon!

Poem 05 / 09

Diamonds

No not Diamonds "in" your eyes, Diamonds "are" your eyes! I agree not the notorious Whites but they are the Blacks so Rare, hence I wonder if they can ever be Mine? On the Full Moon Night, just one glare towards the Sky and even the Moon allures its Sparkling Circle Shining through your eyes! Pretending to be glowing Always, Nobody knows they cry White Diamonds Nowadays! Definitely those Black Diamonds have a dreamy Fairy Tale to Share, but I feel Concealing it is what makes them so Rare!
~Tejaswini
Poem 06 / 09

Betrayal

One heartbeat away from her, One blink away from seeing her, One breath away from reaching her, One dimension away from reaching her Gulping those pills, leaving his human body at midnight, Taking his last breath one last time, Memories with her flashing through his eyes Heavy heart to see his loved ones crying, But happy to leave this "World without her behind" Reaching heaven nowhere he could find her, Asking God where she is? God said, "She is in Hell not in Heaven" "Her betrayal to you was intentional" "Her love for you was pretentious" Being Numb and puzzled, Scars on his heart appearing so faster, Feeling of betrayal flowing through his body like a current No more his innocent heart wants to see her! Realising his mistake, His tearful eyes, pleading God to send him back to life! God says, "Sorry dear"
~Tejaswini K
Poem 07 / 09

Untitled

Chatting with a loved elderly, a memory flashes by to cherish details of it intricately, you close your eyes slowly. Your face lighting up, heart's dancing up, recalling every minute, every second of it. Blurred faces, vague backgrounds Yet reminiscing every breath full of it. Feeling yourself truly present there, in that moment once again experiencing their presence, touch right there. And suddenly, A thunderstorm crashes you away, ripping you out of that moment, felt as an electric shock, numbed your body so fast. Overwhelmed by the depth of that memory, willing to preserve it as a forever treasury. Lifting your wet eyelashes, Finding yourself holding their urn of ashes!
Poem 08 / 09

A Random Day

A cycle ride beneath the rainbow's arc. The stillness of mind when I reach the top of a mountain. The cold breeze kisses my cheeks after a tiring day. The fragrance of the Mogra further refreshes my soul. The hypnotic scent of soil rises after drizzles, followed by a cup of hot coffee after a long trek Familiar yet half forgotten faces staring back from a old album. The hush of night settles in after I have slept through the afternoon. Moonlight warming my skin and the world goes still in midnight. The emptiness of the heart disappearing whenever I look towards this cloudy sky
~Tejaswini
