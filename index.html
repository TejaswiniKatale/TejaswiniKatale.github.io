About

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Houston and a Research Assistant working with Prof. Lu Gao in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering. My research focuses on developing AI-enabled systems for transportation cyber-resilience, aviation safety analytics, and intelligent infrastructure.

My current work explores machine learning, simulation, large language models, and spatio-temporal methods for improving the resilience and safety of transportation systems. I am particularly interested in airport terminal cyber-resilience, ADS-B anomaly detection, and AI-assisted decision support for critical infrastructure.

Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence, transportation systems, and cyber-resilient infrastructure.

Selected Projects

Airport Terminal Cyber-Resilience Simulation

Developed a hybrid simulation framework combining discrete-event simulation, pedestrian movement modeling, and LLM-based passenger behavior to evaluate airport terminal disruptions.

TimesFM-based ADS-B Anomaly Detection

Designed a forecasting-based anomaly detection framework using TimesFM features and a CNN classifier head to detect spoofing, replay, saturation, and interpolation attacks.

Deep Reinforcement Learning for Traffic Signal Control

Implemented a SUMO-based traffic signal control framework using deep Q-learning and partial vehicle detection for intelligent traffic control.

CareBridge: AI-Assisted Telehealth Workflow

Proposed an AI-assisted telehealth workflow for patient intake, injury analysis, doctor recommendation, prescription support, and pharmacy access.
