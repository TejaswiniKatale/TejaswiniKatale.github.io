Publications

Gasoline Prices and Transit Ridership: Evidence from 50 U.S. Transit Agencies, 2002–2026
Transportation Research Record (Under Review), 2026
Bo Chen, Tejaswini Katale, Lu Gao
This study examines how gasoline prices are associated with public transit ridership using monthly panel data from 50 U.S. transit agencies. It finds that higher gasoline prices are linked to increased transit use, with stronger effects for rail than for bus ridership.
Large Language Models in Pavement Safety Analysis: A Comparative Study of Open-Weight Models GitHub
(Poster) ASCE Transportation Conference, 2026
Tejaswini Katale, Gowtham Gudla Reddy, Lu Gao, Nasir Gharaibeh
This study compares open-weight large language models for understanding pavement-related crash narratives. It evaluates how well different models identify pavement condition, roadway context, and safety-relevant information from crash reports.
Pavement Texture Distributions and Wet-Weather Roadway-Departure Crashes
(Presentation) Transportation Research Board (TRB) 2027 Annual Meeting
Nasir Gharaibeh, Yussuf Tarnini, Mohammad Talebzadeh, Boni Kutela, Jingran Sun, Tejaswini Katale
Collaborated with Texas A&M Transportation Institute (TTI). This study examines whether pavement texture is associated with wet-weather roadway-departure crash risk. It explores how roadway surface characteristics can help explain safety outcomes under rainy conditions.
Enhancing Pavement Safety in Texas: Crash Susceptibility Modeling, GIS Tool, and Hydroplaning Risk Assessment Project 0-7177
(Technical Report) U.S. Department of Transportation National Transportation Library (ROSA), 2026
Nasir G. Gharaibeh, Lu Gao, Boni Kutela, Yussuf Tarnini, Mohammad Talebzadeh, Tejaswini Katale
Conducted as part of a TxDOT and FHWA-sponsored pavement safety project in collaboration with the Texas A&M Transportation Institute (TTI). This report integrates pavement condition records, crash data, susceptibility modeling, GIS-based roadway screening, and hydroplaning risk analysis to support proactive identification of high-risk roadway segments in Texas.
Exploring OSHA Construction Accident Records with Multimodal AI Models Paper
Construction Research Congress (CRC) 2026, ASCE
Islem Sahraoui, Tejaswini Katale, Gowtham Reddy Gudla, Lu Gao, Kinam Kim, Zia Din, Ahmed Senouci
This study explores multimodal AI methods for analyzing OSHA construction accident records using both textual narratives (LLM's) and visual information (VLMs). It examines how AI can extract safety-relevant information from accident data to support construction hazard analysis and prevention.
Domain-Adapted AI for Pavement Safety Analysis
(Poster) ICTD, 2026
Tejaswini Katale, Lu Gao, Nasir Gharaibeh
This work evaluates open-weight LLMs on more than 25,000 construction accident narratives for domain-specific hazard classification. It studies 4 hazard groups and 43 categories, with the strongest models achieving F1 scores up to 0.98.
Integrating Pavement Condition Records with LLM-Based Crash Narrative Analysis for Pavement Safety Assessment Paper
Accident Analysis & Prevention, Elsevier, 2026
Sarayu Varma Gottimukkala, Lu Gao, Nasir Gharaibeh, Yussuf Tarnini, Mohammad Talebzadeh, Boni Kutela, Tejaswini Katale
This study integrates pavement condition records with LLM-based crash narrative analysis to identify relationships between roadway surface conditions and crash risk. It combines structured pavement data with information extracted from crash narratives to support more comprehensive pavement safety assessment.
LLM-Guided Hybrid Simulation for Airport Cyber-Resilience Assessment Paper
Mathematics, 2026
Special Issue: Mathematical Methods in System Engineering Modeling and Simulation
Tejaswini Katale, Lu Gao, Yongxin Liu, Dahai Liu, Hongyun Chen
This work integrates LLM-guided passenger decision-making with hybrid airport simulation to evaluate cyber-disruption scenarios. It studies how disruptions affect passenger flow, throughput, delay, and terminal clearance time.
Developing an AI Assistant for Knowledge Management and Workforce Training in State DOTs Paper
Electronics, 2026
Special Issue: Artificial Intelligence-Driven Emerging Applications
Divija Amaram, Lu Gao, Gowtham Gudla Reddy, Tejaswini Katale
This work develops a multi-agent RAG assistant for retrieving knowledge from more than 500 State DOT reports. It combines source-grounded retrieval with multimodal understanding of figures, charts, and tables to support workforce training and transportation knowledge access.
A Bilevel Optimization Framework for Adversarial Control of Gas Pipeline Operations Paper
Actuators, 2025
Tejaswini Katale, Lu Gao, Yunpeng Zhang, Alaa Senouci
This work develops a bilevel optimization framework to study adversarial control of gas pipeline operations. It models the interaction between an attacker and system operator to evaluate operational disruption, optimal response strategies, and infrastructure resilience.
Recession Forecasting: Domain-Specific Feature Selection Methodology Paper
2024 First International Conference for Women in Computing (InCoWoCo)
Best Paper Award
Tejaswini Katale, Jayashri Hase, Aakash Chotrani, Sakshi Ozarde
This work develops a domain-specific feature selection methodology for recession forecasting using economically meaningful indicators. It evaluates how targeted feature selection can improve forecasting performance while identifying the variables most relevant to economic downturns.
