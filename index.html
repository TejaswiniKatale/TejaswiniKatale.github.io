About

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Houston and a Research Assistant working with Prof. Lu Gao in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering. My research focuses on developing AI-enabled systems for transportation cyber-resilience, aviation safety analytics, and intelligent infrastructure.

My current work explores machine learning, simulation, and large language models for improving the resilience and safety of transportation systems. I am particularly interested in anomaly detection, airport terminal cyber-resilience, spatio-temporal modeling, and decision-support systems for critical infrastructure.

Selected Projects

Airport Terminal Cyber-Resilience Simulation

Developed a hybrid simulation framework combining discrete-event simulation, pedestrian movement modeling, and LLM-based passenger behavior to evaluate airport terminal resilience under cyber-induced service disruptions.

TimesFM-based ADS-B Anomaly Detection

Designed a machine learning framework using TimesFM forecasting features and a CNN classifier head to detect spoofing, replay, saturation, and interpolation attacks in aircraft trajectory data.

Deep Reinforcement Learning for Traffic Signal Control

Implemented a SUMO-based traffic signal control system using deep Q-learning and partial vehicle detection to study intelligent traffic signal control.

CareBridge: AI-Assisted Telehealth Workflow

Proposed an AI-assisted telehealth workflow integrating patient intake, injury analysis, doctor recommendation, prescription support, and pharmacy access.
