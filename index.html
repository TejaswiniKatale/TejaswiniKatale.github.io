Tejaswini Katale

Computer Science M.S. Student at University of Houston

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Houston and a Research Assistant working with Prof. Lu Gao in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering.

My research focuses on AI/ML for transportation systems, cyber-resilience, aviation safety analytics, and intelligent infrastructure. In particular, I work on developing machine learning and simulation-based methods for understanding and improving the resilience of transportation systems under disruptions.

My research works include:

  • Airport terminal cyber-resilience using hybrid simulation models that combine service operations, pedestrian movement, and LLM-based passenger behavior.
  • ADS-B anomaly detection using TimesFM-based forecasting features and deep learning classifiers for aviation cybersecurity.
  • Spatio-temporal learning for transportation safety analytics, crash risk modeling, and intelligent infrastructure systems.
  • Large language models and retrieval-augmented generation for knowledge management and AI-assisted decision support.
  • Reinforcement learning for intelligent traffic signal control using SUMO-based simulation environments.

Prior to my graduate studies, I completed my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from Vishwakarma Institute of Technology, Pune, where I worked on projects across machine learning, cybersecurity, healthcare analytics, and intelligent systems.

Feel free to get in touch if you would like to discuss research, collaborate, or connect :)
